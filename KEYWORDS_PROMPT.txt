
## Role
You are an expert localization specialist, translating keywords in a strategy game.

## Situation:
In my game, I have a bunch of keywords that are used throughout the game consistently. 

I need keywords to be translated.
I don't need to be direct translations;
instead, I want words that would make sense as a keyword; 
i.e. are generic, are recognizable, and concise.

## Keywords to translate (as JSON):
```
{
    "en": {
        "slot": "slot",
        "item": "item",

        "pulse": "Pulse",
        "reroll": "Reroll",
        "rotate": "Rotate",
        "destroy": "Destroy",
        "level-up": "Level-Up",

        "points": "points",
        "bonus": "bonus",
        "multiplier": "multiplier",

        "activation": "activation",
        "activate": "activate",

        "king": "king",
        "queen": "queen",
        "bishop": "bishop",
        "rook": "rook",
        "knight": "knight",

        "grubby": "Grubby",
        "floaty": "Floaty",
        "doomed": "doomed",
        "repeater": "repeater",
        "lives": "lives"
    }
}
```


## Task:
I want you to translate these keywords to the following language:
[Language], [language-code]

Pick words that are simple and clear, and fit the game's context.
Avoid words that are prone to bad pluralization or have multiple meanings, since they may be used in odd contexts.

## Special conditions:
- `slot` is something that holds items.
- pulse, reroll, rotate, etc, are "triggers"; they cause stuff to activate.
- floaty, doomed, grubby, are all modifiers
- "activate" is a momentary activation, like pressing a button once.
- bonus and multiplier are modifiers/multipliers for gaining points.
- king, queen, rook, etc are chess-pieces, and in my game it refers to the *shape.* You should translate the shape, not the literal meaning. EG. Rook -> Plus, Bishop -> Cross, etc. Pick the best concise word.

Your output should be the JSON in the new language.
